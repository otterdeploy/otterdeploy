otterdeploy: brand sheet

The mark is a slashed zero: the ring is the product's own rounded-lg node silhouette, the counter is the slash DESIGN.md names as the detail that keeps 0 and O apart. It reads as the "o" of otterdeploy, a zero, and a node: one shape doing three jobs.

Every asset below is generated: brand/scripts/build.sh

Status

The ring never changes. It is the brand. The counter does, and it changes shape, not just hue: at 16px in a tab strip, and for a colour-blind operator, colour alone carries nothing. deploying keeps the idle slash and sends a segment of light travelling the ring, the same vocabulary the project graph uses for pending work; under prefers-reduced-motion that segment parks instead of moving.

Colour

Two inks and one accent, nothing else. The dark accent is the brighter cut because --primary's dark value only reaches 2.1:1 on the dark canvas: fine behind white button text, unreadable as a standalone mark.

Usage

do

  • Use <OtterdeployMark /> and <OtterdeployLogo /> in the app: they track theme tokens automatically.
  • Keep the ring on currentColor so the mark inherits its surface.
  • Give it one ring-corner of clear space on every side.
  • Drop to the mark alone below ~120px of available width.
  • Regenerate with brand/scripts/build.sh after any geometry change.

don't

  • Recolour the slash to anything but the accent, or add a second hue.
  • Fill the ring, add a drop shadow, or seat it on a coloured tile in-product.
  • Set the wordmark in anything but Geist Semibold at −0.025em.
  • Rotate, skew, outline, or reverse the slash direction: it is a /, never a \.
  • Hand-edit anything in brand/logo/ or apps/web/public/: both are build output.
