otterdeploy — logo concepts

Five directions, each held to DESIGN.md: warm-neutral ink (never pure black), one Signal Blue and nothing else chromatic, the same rounded-lg geometry the product's cards and nodes already use. Every mark is drawn on a 32×32 grid, so what you see at 16px is exactly what the favicon will be. Judge them at 16px first — that is where marks die.

Pick one (or one to refine) and the full kit gets generated from it: favicon.svg + .ico + PNG set, apple-touch-icon, maskable PWA icons, OG/social card, a <Logo /> React component to replace the placeholder os tile in auth-layout.tsx, and monochrome + horizontal-lockup variants.
