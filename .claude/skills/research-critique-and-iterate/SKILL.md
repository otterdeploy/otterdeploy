## Operating loop

- Evaluate a screen against a concrete item or answer the user is trying to find. **Fails when:** Linear reading experiences may require sustained comprehension instead of rapid lookup. ([scannable-ui](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=neE6wOuBIP8&t=316s))
- Judge alignment, grouping and visual substitutions by whether they reduce search effort. **Fails when:** Faster recognition alone does not establish that a task is correctly understood. ([scannable-ui](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=neE6wOuBIP8&t=316s))
- Start with inputs, not an empty canvas: define the page or component problem, gather references, and open the design tool only once you have material to react to. Fails when collecting becomes procrastination or a substitute for product definition. ([Iterate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=0s))
- Treat the first version as disposable. Produce a rough complete pass, critique it, and improve it through several passes; keep iterations private when only one polished delivery may be visible. ([Iterate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=0s))
- Optimise deliberate practice for finished-enough volume, not endless polish on one artefact. Fails for paid or portfolio deliverables where one weak artefact is the entire outcome. ([Iterate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=0s))
- Separate the quality of a draft from your ability: treat weak early work as process evidence, not a personal verdict. ([Iterate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=0s))
- Stop when you are recycling the same decisions. Switch tasks or sessions, then return with distance; do not let switching become avoidance that prevents completion. ([Iterate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=0s))
- Interpret a mid-file stall before acting: return to research when the concept lacks direction, but resolve the issue in place when it is merely a craft decision such as spacing or type. ([Mood boards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=404s))
- Preserve dated versions instead of overwriting them, and measure progress by the concrete difference between earlier and current work. Fails when mature work intentionally pursues stable stylistic consistency. ([Progress](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=456s))
- Describe earlier work neutrally through changed dimensions—scale, contrast, density, motion, hierarchy—instead of calling it “wrong.” ([Progress](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=456s))

## Research before design

- Dedicate a bounded research block before substantial design work; skip or shorten it for small changes inside an established system where direction is already fixed. ([Mood boards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=404s))
- Collect several candidate directions and compare them before committing. Stop at a time limit so exploration does not outrun execution. ([Mood boards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=404s))
- Define the concept far enough to describe the whole page and its message sequence, not merely the first section. ([Mood boards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=404s))
- Build a concentrated swipe file of roughly twenty examples of the exact section or screen type you need; reduce the count when a smaller deeply studied set answers the problem. ([Practice plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=491s))
- Search references at the level of the unresolved unit—hero, pricing, testimonial, empty state, transition—instead of browsing entire showcases without a question. Reconcile borrowed sections to one grid and type scale afterward. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Use curated galleries and libraries to expand the option set, but never let trend-led work override the product’s content or audience. ([Mood boards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=404s))
- Keep a searchable screenshot library organised by section, screen, flow, industry, and interaction type. Consult a large sample, then build; collection without annotation or execution does not count as practice. ([Replication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=156s))
- Capture complete unfamiliar flows, including errors, empty states, and alternate paths, rather than saving only the attractive opening screen. Fails when surface references replace defining your own content and structure. ([Pattern research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HE4rLEQpiXY&t=132s))
- Study adjacent products, direct competitors, and existing brand surfaces before setting a redesign direction. ([AI redesign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pGYLZyBE32o&t=0s))
- Compare two or three strong category examples and name the mechanism they share; borrow the mechanism, not their surface styling. ([Audience research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=0s))
- Begin a redesign by naming weaknesses in comparable pages and defining the exact page and outcome you intend to improve. ([AI redesign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pGYLZyBE32o&t=0s))

## Annotate and reverse-engineer references

- Analyse references through proximity, contrast, consistency, and hierarchy; record the reading order, zones, groupings, gaps, dimensions, and alignment rather than saying only that something “looks clean.” ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s))
- Annotate a few references deeply instead of marking up everything shallowly. Capture reusable rhythms and ratios rather than treating screenshot pixels as universal values. ([Practice plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=491s))
- Use substitution to expose hidden logic: replace a successful choice with a deliberately weaker alternative and observe what fails. ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s))
- Evaluate references at two zoom levels: inspect the whole composition, then verify that every label, card, control, and data point still makes sense independently. ([Dashboard audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yr2uIcFZDDQ&t=0s))
- Treat recurring section layouts as a finite decision menu. Choose arrangement, alignment, and visual treatment in that order, then resolve each fork according to content intent. Fails when the brief genuinely requires a novel, brand-defining structure. ([Replication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=156s))
- Use familiar page skeletons as hypotheses, not mandates. A standard SaaS sequence can run hero → adoption proof → explanation → features → experience proof → CTA → footer, but change it when the content cannot earn that progression. ([Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))

## Replicate deliberately

- Rebuild admired interfaces exactly during private practice: match spacing, hierarchy, type, alignment, padding, and component dimensions rather than reproducing only the mood. Never ship or present the clone as original work. ([Replication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=156s))
- Measure and record the real values while replicating, then extract the underlying scale or ratio so the lesson transfers beyond one viewport. ([Replication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=156s))
- Practise one site’s hero and feature section together to learn system consistency, then replicate a structurally different site to prevent one pattern becoming your only answer. ([Practice plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=491s))
- Apply the tension deliberately: copy exactly when learning execution; when designing production work, modify every borrowed pattern to answer your own audience, message, and constraints. ([Practice plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=491s), [Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))
- Restyle one competent layout under at least two opposing practice briefs while holding its structure fixed. Stop holding structure fixed when the target tone or product density genuinely requires a different information model. ([Style practice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=315s))
- Translate each practice brief into concrete variables—imagery, palette, radius, type, control scale, texture, and density—rather than relying on vague adjectives such as “clean” or “modern.” ([Style practice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=315s))
- Sequence self-teaching as perceive → replicate → vary → apply; skip a stage only when the learner already demonstrates that capability. ([Practice plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=491s))
- Bound practice into fixed periods with named outputs, such as annotated references, exact clones, contrasting restyles, or tested wireframes. ([Practice plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=491s))
- Delay portfolio packaging during the earliest learning period so effort stays on capability. If a job, client, or course imposes a deadline, publish the best available work and plan to replace it. ([Progress](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=456s), [Practice plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=491s))
- Benchmark yourself against designers with comparable experience, work type, and market context rather than context-free showcase work. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))

## Build mood boards and structural collages

- Assemble a page-scale reference collage by selecting a useful structural precedent for each section and stacking the sections in message order. ([Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))
- State at least one audience- or content-driven modification for every borrowed section. ([Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))
- Delete the borrowed visual styling once the sequence works; retain only boxes, hierarchy, and order as a wireframe so incompatible source systems do not leak into the design. ([Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))
- Zoom out until the whole page fits and inspect section-to-section rhythm, scale, and coherence before refining any one section. ([Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))
- Separate layout and surface treatment into distinct passes, while recognising that some products—such as dense tools versus playful onboarding—require structural differences rather than cosmetic reskins. ([Style practice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=315s))
- Vary section structure across long marketing pages—stacked, full-bleed, offset, multi-column—without making every section novel. Preserve uniformity where comparison or documentation demands scanning consistency. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Spend novelty selectively: use unconventional structure where it improves the content, and rely on convention elsewhere. Fails for pure showcases where spatial experimentation is itself the deliverable. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))

## Test at low fidelity

- Create deliberately rough wireframes before high-fidelity screens so structural alternatives remain cheap to generate and discard. ([Wireframes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t7mpEDXzjCg&t=340s))
- Use boxes, simple shading, arrows, and notes to communicate layout and behaviour; do not polish a wireframe until it begins masquerading as a finished interface. ([Wireframes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t7mpEDXzjCg&t=340s))
- Test a proposed layout or flow with a few representative people before completing the prototype, revise what observation disproves, and test again. ([Wireframes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t7mpEDXzjCg&t=340s))
- Connect each research or testing finding to the wireframe decision it created, removed, or changed. ([Wireframes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t7mpEDXzjCg&t=340s))
- Preserve only early sketches that clarify a decision or iteration when documenting the work; omit process debris that adds no explanatory value. ([Wireframes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t7mpEDXzjCg&t=340s))
- Stop at validating structure, hierarchy, navigation, and broad task comprehension. Hand specialised branching, verification, onboarding, checkout, or journey design to the appropriate usability-flow skill rather than inventing policy here. ([Style and flows](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=315s))

## Audit before redesigning

- Run diagnosis as a separate pass before proposing changes. Name each defect and preserve the elements that already work; do not use an audit as an indefinite delay. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s), [Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Audit layout, imagery, shape language, typography, copy, navigation, colour, motion, responsiveness, accessibility, and content separately so each criticism becomes actionable. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Score the result on correctness—usable, legible, responsive, on-spec—and memorability—distinctive enough to describe later. Treat low memorability as a defect on attention-driven surfaces, but not on utility-first tools where predictability is the goal. ([Memorability](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SfX43uIubj4&t=0s))
- Require aesthetics, persuasion, and usability simultaneously for commercial pages; visual novelty alone cannot compensate for a failure on the other axes. ([Restraint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=246s))
- Treat functional correctness as the floor. Accept a merely functional result only for internal tools, early MVPs, or disposable prototypes where speed explicitly outranks craft. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Judge whether the page communicates a concrete proposition, not merely whether it is clean and error-free. Exempt intentional single-purpose splash pages. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))
- Rebuild from an empty canvas when the information structure or component logic is fundamentally wrong; prefer targeted fixes when the shipped system’s structure remains sound. ([Dashboard audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yr2uIcFZDDQ&t=0s))
- Set cohesion as a redesign criterion: enforce one spacing rhythm, type scale, shape language, palette logic, and structural model from top to bottom. Do not expand a targeted fix into an unauthorised systemic rewrite. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))

## Diagnose hierarchy and composition

- Repair grouping with proximity before adding size, weight, or colour. Do not tighten elements that are genuine peers, because closeness would imply a false relationship. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))
- Use a stepped spacing scale inside text-and-action clusters: keep the most related elements closest and progressively loosen the gaps toward secondary relationships and actions. Treat example pixel values as scale-specific, not universal. ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s))
- Treat whitespace as finite: add enough to establish focus, then stop before related elements cease to read as a group. Dense interfaces may fail in the opposite direction. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))
- Scale display type and surrounding whitespace together; avoid combining extreme headline scale with already-loose composition unless isolation is the explicit poster-like goal. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))
- Create an unmistakable heading/body distinction through size, weight, line-height, letter-spacing, and spacing—not additional decoration. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Keep one coherent type scale across the page. Allow systematic caption, footnote, and footer tiers, but investigate abrupt mid-page scale changes as seams between incompatible designs. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))
- Use one typeface family as the safe baseline and create hierarchy within it. Introduce a display/body pairing only when it is deliberate, supported, and more useful than the simpler system. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Create hierarchy in compact metadata pairs with contrast before size, but keep essential secondary text above accessibility contrast thresholds. ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s))
- Keep hero text concise, with one concrete promise and short support copy; relax the limit for documentation, editorial, technical, or already-committed readers. ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s), [Audience research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=0s))
- Remove words that repeat information already visible in nearby logos, labels, or interface imagery. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Give hero copy a restrained footprint and protect enough negative space for it to read as a focal group rather than a wall. ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s))
- Let a single-statement section use large type, isolation, and generous space; do not apply that treatment to long copy or multi-item sections. ([Replication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=156s))
- Demote feature copy when a visual genuinely demonstrates the feature; let text lead when the feature is abstract, technical, contractual, or otherwise invisible. ([Replication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=156s))
- Replace overflowing feature rows with a vertical grid when many items must remain discoverable. Avoid mixed tile sizes when all items are genuinely equal because they would invent a false hierarchy. ([Replication](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=156s))
- Cut page length to match actual substance instead of manufacturing scroll with spacing. Exempt documentation and long-form editorial whose depth is real. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))
- Fix information architecture before styling when a section only looks alien because its content does not fit the page’s structural model. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))

## Diagnose audience, imagery, and content

- Test audience specificity by swapping the named audience mentally: if every visual asset could remain unchanged, the page carries too little audience evidence. ([Audience research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=0s))
- Align imagery, vocabulary, examples, iconography, and colour with the named audience’s real world. Use job-to-be-done framing instead for genuinely horizontal products. ([Audience research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=0s))
- Include credible imagery of real users or their environment when contextual recognition builds trust. Omit it when the only available imagery is generic, inaccurate, or visibly stock-like. ([Audience research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=0s))
- Investigate an imagery-free marketing page as a likely defect, while allowing deliberate text-only systems supported by exceptional typography and layout. ([Audience research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=0s))
- Resolve the imagery tension by page purpose: show the actual product when understanding the product is the conversion lever; show the customer’s environment when recognition and audience trust are the lever. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s), [Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))
- Secure primary imagery before refining an image-led layout. Use placeholders only when imagery is secondary and will not determine composition. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Reserve the focal region of hero imagery and place text around it. If overlay is unavoidable, add a scrim, gradient, blur, or contained text surface that restores contrast. ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s))
- Select images with quiet regions where copy must sit; reject an unsuitable asset before compensating with excessive text effects. ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s))
- Let product-led hero photography remain large and uninterrupted. Use cutouts or collage only as a systematic editorial motif, not an isolated decoration. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Use compact social-proof signals above the fold and place full testimonials later, unless one endorsement is intentionally the page’s central proposition. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Pair adoption proof such as logos with experience proof such as testimonials when both are available; they answer different trust questions. ([Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))
- Validate placeholder prices, thresholds, discounts, dates, and statistics against plausible category values before review. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Redirect a polished-but-hollow page upstream to audience research and positioning instead of adding more visual effects. ([Audience research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=0s))

## Diagnose navigation, colour, shape, and motion

- Keep navigation quiet, balanced, and hierarchical: emphasise one primary action, elevate only the most important links, and mute the rest. Give denser application shells the weight their navigation task requires. ([Teardown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=0s), [Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Keep the user’s location legible through persistent navigation where appropriate, visible current state, and predictable structure. ([Restraint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=246s))
- Add page indexes or section scrollers only when page length or scroll narrative creates a real orientation problem. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Move social links out of commerce headers because they exit the funnel; retain them when following or community participation is the intended conversion. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Build an explicit interface palette for surfaces, text tiers, borders, and accents rather than relying on photography to supply all colour. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Apply accent colour only where attention is intended; widespread accent use destroys its signalling value. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Keep radius and shape language systematic across controls, cards, containers, imagery, and icons. Permit mixed shapes only when each category follows a deliberate rule. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Test abstract decorations for unintended objects, symbols, or comic readings before shipping; allow ambiguity when playfulness is appropriate. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Default conversion-oriented work to restrained layout, typography, contrast, hierarchy, and limited motion. Spend heavily on spectacle only when awareness or memorability is the primary goal. ([Restraint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=246s))
- Resolve the expression tension explicitly: add visual interest when an attention-driven page feels generic, but preserve straightforward low-distraction treatment when clarity and task completion dominate. ([Visual interest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ulSOdTgoGeY&t=0s))
- Animate a minority of elements with small amplitude and clear purpose. Permit pervasive motion only when motion itself is the deliverable. ([Progress](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=456s))
- Reduce maximalist work by shrinking excessive type, removing animated elements, and reserving remaining motion for hierarchy. Do not neutralise intentionally expressive brand work into generic restraint. ([Progress](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WVt1CelBfg&t=456s))
- Remove any effect that measurably harms loading, navigation, readability, responsiveness, or control. ([Restraint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=246s))
- Treat default hover colour changes as basic state feedback, not a complete motion system; add intentional transitions only where they improve feedback or attention. ([Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Let decorative sticky elements scroll away; keep only navigation, state, progress, cart, or other continuously useful controls persistent. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))
- Verify every layout across the supported viewport range as part of the base design, not as final polish. ([Restraint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=246s))

## Select resources deliberately

- Assign recurring non-core weaknesses to specialised tools when doing so removes a repeated delivery bottleneck. Do not outsource the central discipline of the engagement or use tools to avoid learning forever. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Prefer assets that paste into Figma as editable vectors or layers. Choose code exports instead when runtime behaviour is the asset’s essential value. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Use a pairing engine to generate typography candidates, then keep human judgement as the veto. Follow existing brand requirements and verify language coverage first. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Use a clearly marked placeholder logo so unresolved identity work does not block layout; never let the placeholder escape into final delivery. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Choose lightweight motion tools that resemble the static design environment for micro-interactions; use full motion-graphics software for complex compositing or long-form work. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Generate mesh or multi-stop gradients in a dedicated tool when the design application cannot produce them efficiently; account for raster resolution, file weight, and lack of theme responsiveness. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Choose parametric icon sets and match their stroke, radius, and style to the interface. Review all candidates together before export so optical size and metaphor stay coherent. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Reuse mature, shipped micro-interaction patterns before inventing new ones, but adapt timing and personality to the current product and omit delight where speed matters more. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Judge stock libraries by findability and relevance rather than catalogue size. Use less-common sources for differentiation only after verifying commercial rights. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Evaluate resource quality through both its own product craft and the depth of its actual assets; a polished marketing site can conceal a thin or abandoned library. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Start time-constrained multi-page work from one coherent kit or component library, then retype, recolour, and restructure it enough to carry the product’s identity. Avoid mixing several systems without normalising their primitives. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s), [Reference collage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=185s))
- Treat recurring arrows, connectors, and annotation marks as reusable assets rather than redrawing them inconsistently. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Replace lifeless mid-fidelity placeholders with coherent interactive components when missing affordance and detail are the problem; do not use borrowed components to conceal unresolved structure. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Build literacy in AI-interface patterns for uncertainty, correction, and trust, while avoiding trend cues that do not fit the product’s positioning. ([Tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eeN7yUcIWbw&t=0s))
- Use contemporary AI styling such as soft gradients, subtle noise, and rounded components only as a candidate direction, not an automatic final art direction. Reject it when a utilitarian, restrained, or durable identity fits better. ([AI redesign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pGYLZyBE32o&t=0s))

## Maintain Figma hygiene

- Name layers consistently and place repeated groups at the same relative hierarchy position so matching and bulk selection remain reliable. Avoid generic repeated names that could over-select unrelated objects. ([Figma bulk edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fL1X2Mw6s3w&t=0s))
- Bound bulk operations inside a selected section or visible scope before editing so the blast radius is inspectable. Do not reorganise the file merely to support one temporary operation when the intended frames are genuinely scattered. ([Figma bulk edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fL1X2Mw6s3w&t=0s))
- Select all true instances of a repeated element and apply shared visual, positional, or textual changes once. Do not bulk-edit elements whose similarity conceals intentional differences. ([Figma bulk edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fL1X2Mw6s3w&t=0s))
- Apply auto layout to compatible frames in one operation so their structural treatment remains identical. Separate frames with genuinely different internal arrangements first. ([Figma bulk edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fL1X2Mw6s3w&t=0s))
- Reposition repeated elements through shared selection and auto layout instead of nudging copies individually. ([Figma bulk edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fL1X2Mw6s3w&t=0s))
- Replace repeated placeholder or shared copy in one multi-selection operation, but preserve instances containing meaningful content variation. ([Figma bulk edit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fL1X2Mw6s3w&t=0s))

## Produce an ordered improvement plan

- Convert every diagnosed defect into a specific change, evidence source, expected effect, and verification method. ([Site audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V3Omp1hm0Sg&t=0s))
- Order fixes upstream to downstream: audience and positioning → content and information architecture → page structure → hierarchy and spacing → imagery and typography → colour and shape → motion and polish. ([Audience research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCneB_MQ7qs&t=0s), [Landing critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eMMiLeo_UGI&t=0s))
- Prioritise defects that block comprehension, navigation, accessibility, or conversion before defects of memorability or polish. Reverse that priority only when the experience itself is the product. ([Restraint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=246s))
- Preserve successful elements explicitly so iteration does not discard solved decisions along with weak ones. ([Product critique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvbFPzLjWcU&t=0s))
- Validate each iteration at page scale, component scale, supported viewport sizes, and—where structure or interaction changed—with lightweight user observation. ([Dashboard audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yr2uIcFZDDQ&t=0s), [Wireframes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t7mpEDXzjCg&t=340s))
- Stop when the design meets the brief’s purpose and the remaining changes are preference rather than defect; do not use elaborate effects as the measure of skill or completeness. ([Restraint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CC8lLnqa28&t=246s))
